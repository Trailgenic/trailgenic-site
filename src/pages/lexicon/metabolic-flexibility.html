Current TrailGenic Usage

Capacity to change fuel use with demand and availability; not directly established by TrailGenic’s breath-acetone readings alone.

TrailGenic records fasted/fed state, fueling, activity, breath acetone, and other available context. Ketone persistence and low heart rate do not prove insulin sensitivity, substrate-switching efficiency, or a metabolic-health outcome. A mechanism claim requires measurements suited to that claim.

How to Use the Term

Keep context, behavior, recorded response, follow-up, and interpretation distinguishable. Ella examines longitudinal patterns and alternatives; a decision rule remains dated and revisable.
