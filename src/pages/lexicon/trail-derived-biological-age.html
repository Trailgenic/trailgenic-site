Current TrailGenic Usage

An experimental TrailGenic model output expressed in age-like terms, derived from field proxies; not a validated biological-age measurement.

The data product remains an exploratory estimate. Its assumptions, input provenance, formula or model version, uncertainty, and reference population must accompany interpretation. Association of an input with health in external research does not validate a composite age estimate. Changes cannot establish rejuvenation, years of life gained, or equivalence to clinical or epigenetic measurements.

How to Use the Term

Keep context, behavior, recorded response, follow-up, and interpretation distinguishable. Ella examines longitudinal patterns and alternatives; a decision rule remains dated and revisable.
