Current TrailGenic Usage

The vertical component of a hiking task, described through gain, descent, grade, duration, and altitude context.

Elevation gain is not the same as peak altitude, and neither alone measures physiological strain. Link the route profile and actual dose to heart rate, pace, symptoms, and recovery observations. Comparisons across routes retain terrain, conditions, stops, and prior training as possible explanations.
