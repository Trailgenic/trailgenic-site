Current TrailGenic Usage

A longitudinal display or hypothesis about changing response across repeated, comparable observations.

An adaptation curve must name its metric, protocol, comparison window, and missingness. It may show improvement, stability, worsening, or uncertainty. A smooth trend alone does not establish a mechanism; route, pace, environment, device estimates, and training history remain possible explanations.

How to Use the Term

Keep context, behavior, recorded response, follow-up, and interpretation distinguishable. Ella examines longitudinal patterns and alternatives; a decision rule remains dated and revisable.
