Current TrailGenic Usage

Hiking with a documented period since caloric intake; an observed protocol context rather than a required entry to TrailGenic.

Record timing, actual intake, duration, intensity, environment, symptoms, and follow-up. A fasted observation does not establish superiority to a fed protocol, substrate use, autophagy, or a longevity effect. Foundation practice can begin without fasting; compare declared fasted and fed conditions only where suitable observations exist.

How to Use the Term

Keep context, behavior, recorded response, follow-up, and interpretation distinguishable. Ella examines longitudinal patterns and alternatives; a decision rule remains dated and revisable.
