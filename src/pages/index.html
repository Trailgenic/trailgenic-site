01 / The Mission

Longevity on
our own terms.

TrailGenic is a movement-based longevity and adaptation system built around repeatable practice, measured response, and renewable capacity. Its protocols and longitudinal field datasets connect what people do with how their bodies respond over time. Ella helps interpret those observations, examine patterns, and inform practical decisions.

Discipline does not require wealth. The practice begins on flat ground: repeat the movement, respect recovery, and learn from what changes over time.

Our aim is to help people pursue:

  • Cardiovascular efficiency
  • Metabolic flexibility
  • Sleep quality
  • Recovery readiness
  • Energy stability
  • Body composition
  • Cognitive clarity
  • Durable capacity

Walk, ruck, run, hike, recover, and learn. The body still speaks. TrailGenic gives the signal structure.

02 / The Founding Experience

One lived case.
A reason to begin.

In 2023, Mike Ye was told he would likely need blood pressure medication long term. His reported blood pressure improved during a sustained practice of movement, hiking, measured recovery, and consistency.

That experience became the origin of TrailGenic: a method built from human practice and examined through longitudinal field data.

Explore hiking →

Foundation Movement: walking, rucking, and running create controlled, repeatable opportunities to observe adaptation on the same flat-ground course. These layers prioritize route and protocol consistency; changing conditions remain possible confounders.

Hiking: the environmental field expression brings terrain, surface, altitude, wind, temperature, metabolic context, and sleep recovery into the record.

Renewable capacity: TrailGenic asks whether comparable or harder work becomes less physiologically costly, creates smaller recovery disruption, shows recovery over follow-up, and remains available for return.

The Six Pillars: Fasted Movement · High-Altitude Training · Adaptation / Longevity Integration · Electrolyte Control · Nature Immersion · Measured Recovery. These are research pillars, not mandatory exposures for every person; heat, cold and wind are observed field conditions. Explore how the pillars apply →

05 / The Evidence

Follow the record.

The September 30 master holds 108 observations across four movement layers: running VO₂ max progresses 45 → 46 → 48, hiking cardiovascular response trends lower at broadly maintained workloads, carried load progresses 10 → 20 lb, and 41 paired hikes show rising breath acetone. Linked recovery windows reveal how these responses unfold. H42 adds a San Jacinto repeat with an injury-affected descent and recovery.
