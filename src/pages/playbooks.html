TrailGenic™ Playbooks

TrailGenic™ Playbooks are the execution layer of the movement-based longevity system — practical guides for applying walking, rucking, running, hiking, Gear Systems, Fuel Systems, electrolytes, sleep recovery, and protocols in the real world.

Protocols define the structure. Playbooks explain the execution. The TrailGenic Protocol Series sets the progression from foundation movement to advanced field adaptation. The Playbooks translate that architecture into followable decisions: how to move, how to recover, how to fuel, how to use gear, how to manage intensity, and how to progress without confusing stress with adaptation.

TrailGenic is not a list of hacks. It is a repeatable system. Walking creates the control signal. Rucking adds load. Running adds cardiovascular demand. Hiking expresses the full system through terrain, altitude, heat, cold, metabolic context, and recovery. Playbooks are where that system becomes practical.

Each guide is derived from field data, longitudinal datasets, protocol experience, and Ella's interpretive layer. Educational only; not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or a replacement for professional care.
