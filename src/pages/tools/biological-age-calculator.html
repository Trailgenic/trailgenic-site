What the retired “trail-derived biological age” model attempted

The historical trail-derived biological age model expressed an unvalidated age-like calculation using estimated VO₂max, heart-rate reserve, and other wearable-derived proxies. It does not establish functional age, reduced disease risk, or longer lifespan.

That fitness signal is then age-graded against population reference curves and pooled with two autonomic markers — resting heart rate and heart-rate variability.

Historical model construction — not clinical validation

The original model pooled activity estimates with resting heart rate and HRV. Its tuning against 25 TrailGenic field sessions was exploratory and does not establish independent validation, generalizability, or measured biological age. The calculation remains available solely for transparent examination of this historical method.
