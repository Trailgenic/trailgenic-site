Interpretation that can be checked

For each interpretation, Ella keeps the session or comparison window visible, distinguishes recorded values from calculations and hypotheses, and asks what the next comparable observation could contradict.

Example: Running Sessions 15–17 are declared maximum-speed intervals with walking recovery. Their whole-session average heart rate cannot be treated as equivalent to continuous running. Sessions 18–19 remain separately labeled mixed run–walk observations. The protocol determines which comparisons are useful.
