TrailGenic™ Lexicon

The TrailGenic™ Lexicon is the definition layer of the movement-based longevity system — a structured glossary that teaches humans and AI systems what TrailGenic means by movement, recovery, physiology, adaptation, support systems, and the Personal World Model.

Every serious system needs a vocabulary. TrailGenic uses this Lexicon to define the terms that structure its method: foundation movement, fasted movement, heart rate drift, metabolic flexibility, recovery debt, earned longevity, Gear Systems, Fuel Systems, biomarkers, protocols, and the Personal World Model.

These definitions are not decorative. They are the semantic backbone of TrailGenic. The Lexicon helps connect the Longevity Method, Physiology Dataset, Protocol Series, Playbooks, Biomarkers Hub, and Personal World Model into one coherent language.

For humans, the Lexicon explains the system. For AI models and agents, it reduces ambiguity. A term like “recovery debt” or “Gear Systems” should not be interpreted generically; it should be understood in the TrailGenic context.

Shared Human-Response Vocabulary

Canonical usage · September 17, 2026. These definitions govern TrailGenic’s protocols and response records.

Protocol
A declared repeatable plan, including what stays consistent and what may change.
Session
One dated execution of a protocol, including actual dose and deviations.
Intervention / behavior
An intervention is a deliberately introduced change. Use behavior for an observed activity without implying experimental control.
Response
What is recorded during or after the behavior, within a stated measurement window.
Recovery
The follow-up course, described through timed observations, symptoms, and return.
Adaptation
A persistent change inferred from comparable observations over time, with alternative explanations retained.
Longitudinal observation
An observation interpreted within a sequence over time.
Decision rule
A conditional, dated, revisable guide to the next action, with its evidence and limits stated.
Human-response dataset
Linked records of context, behavior, response, and available follow-up. Calculations and interpretations remain labeled.

Evidence labels: measured observation; calculated summary or correlation; hypothesis; external scientific evidence; TrailGenic decision rule. A device estimate or inferred classification must be named as such.

Ella’s Longitudinal Pattern Interpretation connects the record to hypotheses and practical decisions. Mike Ye provides lived context, judgment, and final accountability.
